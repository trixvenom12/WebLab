About the Department

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at Mohandas College of Engineering and Technology (MCET) is a two-year postgraduate programme affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU), Kerala.
The programme is designed to develop highly skilled computing professionals capable of addressing the evolving challenges of the global technology ecosystem.
